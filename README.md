<details>
<summary><b>🎙️ Nerd corner: which part of your voice gives a deepfake away?</b></summary>
<br/>

Speech isn't one signal. Vowels are **voiced** (your vocal folds vibrate, so there's a pitch), while sounds like *s*, *f* and *t* are **unvoiced** (just shaped noise). In my research at SETU, Ireland, I split every clip into speech-only, voiced-only and unvoiced-only streams using voice activity detection and pitch tracking:

$$m_u(t) = m_s(t)\,\bigl(1 - m_v(t)\bigr)$$

where $m_s$ marks speech frames and $m_v$ marks voiced frames. Then I trained 5 models on each stream against 13 spoofing attacks and used SHAP and LIME to see which acoustic cues they actually rely on. Code: [Can-You-Hear-The-Music](https://github.com/DevyanshBhattacharya/Can-You-Hear-The-Music).

</details>
